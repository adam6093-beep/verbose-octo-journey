⚡ HUMAN METABOLISM & ATP

Interactive Core Concept Map

1. Definition

  • Anabolism: Building complex molecules Requires ATP
  • Catabolism: Breaking down molecules Releases ATP

2. Macromolecules

  • Carbohydrates: Primary fuel source (Glucose)
  • Lipids: High-density energy storage (Fatty Acids)
  • Proteins: Structural/Functional fuel (Amino Acids)

3. Nutrient Pools

  • Nutrient Pool: Circulating blood nutrients
  • Storage Deposits: Liver & Muscle Glycogen, Adipose tissue
  • Dynamic Balance: Continuous flux between stored & free state

4. ATP Pathways

  • Glycolysis: Cytoplasm (Anaerobic / Aerobic)
  • Citric Acid Cycle: Mitochondrial Matrix
  • Electron Transport: Inner Mitochondrial Membrane

5. Metabolic States

  • Absorptive State: Fed State Insulin Driven
  • Post-Absorptive State: Fasted State Glucagon Driven
